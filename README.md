## Acadmate - Your Academic Companion

Acadmate is a student-run academic resource management system that allows users to upload and download study materials such as previous years’ question papers, books, notes, and other resources to help them prepare for exams efficiently.

For example, a student who has just completed his/her examination could scan their question paper and upload it on our platform with all the details of that specific subject (subject name, branch, year, etc). It would then be easily accessible by all other registered students for download, hence they could get to know the type or level of questions asked in the past, eventually helping them in their preparation.

## Objectives

The main objective of our project is to cater to the academic needs of the
students of our university. Lack of accessibility to proper academic resources is
the problem we want to solve through our web application. Study materials like
handouts, notes, ppts, reference books, practice exercises, and previous years’
question papers are not easily accessible to all the students. Hence, this
web-based student-run platform would serve as a central repository for all the
college study material needs, which would thus help many students.

## Proposed Outcomes

- To learn firebase and Microsoft Azure directory to handle user
authentication using university Microsoft accounts
- To configure & store resources on google firebase cloud storage or AWS s3
- To learn React for making the frontend of our webapp
- To gain knowledge about Express and MongoDB for making the backend of
our platform
- To write clean and structured code to ensure future scalability
