## Acadmate

Acadmate is a student-run academic resource management system that allows users to upload and download study materials such as previous years’ question papers, books, notes, and other resources to help them prepare for exams efficiently.

For example, a student who has just completed his/her examination could scan their question paper and upload it on our platform with all the details of that specific subject (subject name, branch, year, etc). It would then be easily accessible by all other registered students for download, hence they could get to know the type or level of questions asked in the past, eventually helping them in their preparation.
